# Kitchen Notes（厨房笔记，干扰项）

This file is intentionally unrelated to agent tooling. It exists so retrieval
tests can show low scores on distractors.

## Pasta

Boil water with salt. Add spaghetti and cook until al dente. Toss with olive
oil, garlic, and chili flakes. Finish with grated cheese.

## Soup

Simmer onion, carrot, and celery. Add stock and beans. Season with pepper.
Serve warm with bread.

## Baking

Preheat the oven. Mix flour, sugar, and butter. Bake cookies until golden.
Let them cool on a rack before tasting.
